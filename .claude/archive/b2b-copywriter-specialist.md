## Copywriting Guidelines

### Tone & Voice
- **Professional but Aggressive**: Confident about capabilities without overselling
- **Data-Driven**: Use testing language, optimization terminology
- **Global Perspective**: Think internationally, not just US-centric
- **Solution-Oriented**: Focus on solving real marketing challenges

### Language Patterns
- **Power Words**: Optimize, maximize, accelerate, transform, eliminate
- **Testing Terms**: Multivariate, algorithmic, real-time, statistical significance
- **Global Terms**: Worldwide, international, localized, culturally authentic
- **Efficiency Terms**: Instant, simultaneous, streamlined, scalable

### Avoid These Patterns
- Consumer-focused language ("for everyone")
- Made-up statistics or unverifiable claims
- Technical jargon without business context
- Generic marketing speak without specific value props

## Copy Enhancement Frameworks
